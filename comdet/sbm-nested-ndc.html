SBM-nested-ndc

nested (hierarchical) non-degree-corrected stochastic block model

Stacks flat blockmodels into a hierarchy; NDC entropy at every level. The page walks level 0.

Draft, level-0 walker only. Walker for the NDC variant of the nested SBM. The family-level Bayesian framing, the hierarchical construction, the round-robin MCMC, and the cross-algorithm contrasts live on the SBM-nested-best anchor page; this page focuses on the level-0 NDC walker. The kernel runs the full multi-level chain end-to-end and the cpp tracer plus JS replay leg exercise the hierarchy under self-RNG (306/306 cells PASS on the 3-tier stress panel); the page animates only the level-0 trajectory.

The nested non-degree-corrected SBM uses NDC entropy at every level of the hierarchy. The contrast with the nested-dc page is at level 0: nested-DC pays the degree DL term and uses the DC vertex factor \(\log(e_r!)\), while nested-NDC drops the degree DL and uses the NDC vertex factor \(e_r \log n_r\) at level 0. Every upper level is identical: both nested variants use NDC entropy at level \(l > 0\), because the upper-level "vertices" are level-0 blocks with no separate degree sequence.

The description length the chain minimises is

\(\Sigma_{\text{nested-NDC}} = \sum_{l = 0}^{L - 1} \Sigma^{(l)}_{\text{NDC}}\),

where each \(\Sigma^{(l)}_{\text{NDC}}\) is the flat-NDC \(\Sigma\) on the level-\(l\) graph (sparse entropy NDC + edges DL + partition DL; no degree DL at any level). In plain terms: pay the NDC cost at every level of the hierarchy. Two properties. The level-0 NDC entropy carries the hub-leaf penalty the flat-ndc walker exposes; the nested form does not modify this penalty, so the level-0 mode partition under nested-NDC matches the flat-ndc mode partition on the comdet fixture. And the absence of the degree DL term at level 0 simplifies the kernel's bookkeeping compared to nested-DC; both variants run the same round-robin MCMC but differ only in the entropy form at level 0.

The level-0 walker on this page surfaces only the level-0 trajectory. The upper-level contributions to \(\Sigma_{\text{nested-NDC}}\) are computed by the kernel after the round-robin chain converges but are not animated. For a fixture this size the level-1+ contributions are small in absolute nats; the level-0 \(\Sigma\) dominates the total.

On the 32-node fixture the resolution-limit benefit of the nested form is mostly cosmetic. \(\sqrt{32} \approx 5.7\), so the flat SBM can already represent the 4-community ground-truth comfortably. The hierarchy starts paying for itself when the optimal block count exceeds \(\sqrt{N}\). Without degree correction, A's hub at node 0 tends to peel into its own level-0 block: NDC has no way to absorb the degree gap between the \(K_5\) vertices and the periphery leaves under an exchangeable-within-block prior.

stage 1 · random init

Random partition with \(B_0\) blocks; \(\Sigma_0\) on display.

MCMC starts from any valid partition. The level-0 chain initialises with \(B_0 = \) 8 blocks: the kernel shuffles the identity permutation under MT19937 and assigns block \(i \bmod B_0\) to vertex \(\sigma(i)\). The shuffle is backward Fisher-Yates, matching the std::shuffle direction in the cpp tracer.

\(\Sigma_0\) is the level-0 NDC entropy at the random init; the kernel also computes the level-1+ contributions in the background. MCMC's job at level 0 is to push the level-0 \(\Sigma\) down by re-routing nodes; under NDC, the chain has a strong incentive to either group hubs with other hubs or peel them into singletons, both of which lower the \(e_r \log n_r\) penalty.

stage 2 · one move at a time

Each visit: pick a vertex, propose a target block, accept or reject.

One sweep visits every level-0 vertex once in shuffled order. Each visit runs four steps:

  1. Build the candidate pool: every currently non-empty block at level 0, plus one fresh empty slot when the vertex's current block holds more than one member.
  2. Sample a target uniformly from the pool (the \(c \to \infty\) limit of Peixoto's local-neighbourhood proposal).
  3. Compute \(\Delta\Sigma\) for the move via the subset-entropy delta on the level-0 NDC entropy.
  4. Accept with probability \(\min(1, e^{-\beta\Delta\Sigma})\) at \(\beta = 1\). On accept, commit; on reject, leave the partition unchanged.

The \(\Delta\Sigma\) at each visit is the level-0 NDC delta only. The upper-level contributions are deterministic given the level-0 partition; the round-robin pass picks them up between sweeps. The NDC delta has the same closed-form structure as DC's, but the vertex factor is \(e_r \log n_r\) instead of \(\log(e_r!)\); the per-visit \(|\Delta\Sigma|\) values tend to be larger in absolute size than DC's because the NDC vertex factor is sensitive to per-1 changes in \(n_r\).

The kernel composes the full NestedBlockState over the level-0 mode partition: level \(l + 1\) is a flat NDC BlockState on the level-\(l\) block-multigraph (vertices = non-empty level-\(l\) blocks, edges = level-\(l\) \(e_{rs}\) counts). Per-level MCMC and joint multi-level inference exist in the kernel and the cpp tracer; the walker on this page shows only the level-0 trajectory.

how the hierarchy composes

Level 0's mode partition collapses into the level-1 graph: vertices are the non-empty level-0 blocks renumbered \(0 \ldots B_{\text{ne}} - 1\), edges carry the level-0 \(e_{rs}\) counts as weights, and self-loops carry \(e_{rr}/2\) per the doubled-diagonal convention. Level 1 is itself a flat NDC BlockState over that smaller multigraph. The nested total \(\Sigma_{\text{nested-NDC}} = \sum_l \Sigma^{(l)}_{\text{NDC}}\) is self-consistent across levels. The walker on this page animates level 0; the full multi-level \(e_{rs}\) propagation runs end-to-end in the kernel and the cpp tracer matches it byte-for-byte under the 3-tier stress panel.

contrast against the DC sister page

The nested-dc page walks the same fixture with DC entropy at level 0. On A, nested-DC absorbs node 0's degree into the model and reads A as cohesive; nested-NDC pays the hub-leaf penalty and tends to peel the hub. The nested-DC vs. nested-NDC \(\Delta\Sigma\) on this fixture is in the same ballpark as the flat-DC vs. flat-NDC gap. The nested-best page surfaces the comparison directly.
