Same Leiden optimiser, modularity objective \(Q = \frac{1}{2m}\sum_{ij}\big(A_{ij} - \frac{k_i k_j}{2m}\big)\delta(c_i, c_j)\). Inherits modularity's resolution limit: communities below \(\sqrt{2m}\) get absorbed regardless of structure. Useful on small graphs, degrades on large sparse ones where the limit bites hardest.
