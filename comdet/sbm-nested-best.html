Draft, level-0 only. Meta-page over the two nested-SBM variants. Each runs the level-0 MCMC chain (uniform proposal, \(\beta = 1\), 20 sweeps) from the same seeded random init; each variant minimises its own description length under its own priors. \(\Sigma\) values across variants are not directly comparable in absolute terms; the page surfaces the pairwise gap and the per-variant verdict (\(|\Delta\Sigma| < 5\) nats = inconclusive, 5–10 = suggestive, beyond 10 = decisive per Peixoto 2017 §VII.A). The PP variant has no nested counterpart in graph-tool and is omitted. Bit-equality vs. the cpp tracer holds for each variant under multi-level e_rs propagation across the 17-fixture 3-tier stress panel (306/306 cells PASS).

The nested SBM family has two principled variants: degree-corrected and non-degree-corrected. Each makes a different prior assumption about the data, and there is no a-priori reason to pick one over another on an unseen network. The standard recipe is to run both and pick the variant with the lowest description length, treating that choice as a Bayesian model-selection step. That is what nested-best does in run_cd.sh; this page is the same recipe with the comparison surfaced.

DC degree-corrected sparse entropy at level 0; degree-DL prices the per-node degree sequence given block sums. Survives heavy-tailed degree distributions.
NDC non-degree-corrected sparse entropy at every level; the vertex term scales with \(n_r\) instead of \(e_r\). Useful when degree variance carries cluster signal that DC would otherwise absorb.

caveat · \(\Sigma\) across variants is not directly comparable

The two variants compute \(\Sigma\) under different priors and different data-fit terms; the absolute values sit on different scales. Picking the lowest is meaningful when the variants share the same model class (Peixoto 2017 §VII.A) and should be read as "this variant compresses the data better under its own assumptions" rather than a strict ranking. The Bayes-factor odds \(\Lambda = 2^{-\Delta\Sigma}\) applies pairwise when the variants compete as alternative explanations; on the 32-node fixture the gap is usually wide enough that the verdict is stable across seeds.
